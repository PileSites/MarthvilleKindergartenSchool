OUR JOURNEY

The Marthville Story

A dream that became reality in 2026. Follow our journey from vision to vibrant community.

The Inspiration

How it all began!

Marthville was named in honor of the director's late mother, Mrs. Lalobo Martha Auma, whose life was marked by extraordinary love, warmth, and generosity. She welcomed everyone like family and taught values of faith, empathy, inclusiveness, and kindness. The name combines "Marth" from Martha and "Ville" to represent the loving village she created. Marthville stands as a living legacy of her heart, values, and the sense of belonging she gave to others.

Early 2025

The Vision Takes Shape

Nancy dreamed of creating a kindergarten where every child felt truly at home, safe, loved, and free to explore the joy of learning. With that dream firmly in her heart, the early plans for Marthville began to unfold, turning vision into reality.

Looking Ahead

Building Our Dream

We set out to bring together passionate and qualified Ugandan educators who shared our dream of nurturing young minds with love, excellence, and integrity. Guided by Nancy's leadership, our founding team united, each member bringing dedication, expertise, and a shared belief in the Marthville vision.

Setting Up

Assembling Our Team

We began recruiting passionate, qualified Ugandan educators who share our vision for nurturing young minds. Our founding team came together under Nancy's leadership.

Curriculum

Curriculum Development

Our educational team designed a rich, play-based curriculum that blends the best of early childhood research with innovative, child-centered approaches. Every lesson, activity, and learning experience was thoughtfully crafted to nurture curiosity, creativity, and a lifelong love for learning.

Today

Growing Together in Gulu

Today, Marthville continues to flourish as a nurturing home for early childhood education in Gulu. Our community grows stronger each day as we stay true to the values of love, kindness, and excellence that inspired this journey. Every child who walks through our doors leaves their own barefoot print on our hearts.

OUR CLASSES

Meet Our Learning Families

We run four classes with unique names that spark imagination.

🐧

Playful Penguins

DayCare

Playful Penguins is a warm and nurturing daycare designed for babies and toddlers. In a safe, loving environment, children enjoy gentle routines, sensory play, music, and movement that support early development. Our caregivers provide constant care and encouragement, helping little ones build emotional security, social skills, curiosity, and a joyful foundation for lifelong learning.

🐬

Happy Dolphins

Kindergarten 1

Happy Dolphins is a playful kindergarten class where children explore, imagine, and develop early literacy, numeracy, and creative skills through engaging activities. With storytelling, art, music, outdoor play, and teamwork, learners build confidence, communication, and independence in a joyful environment that encourages curiosity and a lasting love for learning.

🐻

Curious Cubs

Kindergarten 2

Curious Cubs nurtures children's natural curiosity through activities that build early literacy, numeracy, creativity, and problem-solving skills. Through stories, art, experiments, music, and purposeful play, learners explore ideas while developing teamwork, confidence, and independence in a supportive and encouraging environment.

🦊

Clever Foxes

Kindergarten 3

Clever Foxes prepares children for the transition to primary school by strengthening literacy, numeracy, critical thinking, and communication skills. Through guided reading, early writing, hands-on learning, and collaborative activities, learners build confidence, independence, and problem-solving abilities in a supportive and engaging environment.
